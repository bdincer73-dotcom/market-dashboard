CMF (Chaikin Money Flow) is a -1 to +1 oscillator, not a percentage — positive means net buying pressure, negative means net selling. It's a different kind of metric from the % stats next to it (breadth, Net New Hi%), which measure share of market.

🟡 Buildingcomposite 40-59
🟠 Elevated FOMOcomposite 60-74
🔴 REDUCE — fragile topcomposite ≥ 75 AND at least 2 of the 3 axes individually above 70
Three axes — Stretch (price over-extension), Leverage (margin debt + suppressed vol), Crowding (concentration + correlation) — combine into a 0-100 composite. RED requires the conjunction of a high composite AND multiple hot axes at once: "expensive but stable" alone won't trigger it, by design.

Net Liquidity = Fed total assets − Treasury General Account − ON RRP, a widely used but unofficial proxy. Score starts at 50 and moves with this week's Net Liquidity change, the 4-week trend, and bank reserves' own 4-week move (when available) — see config/thresholds.yaml for the exact weights. Independent of breadth/technicals: a Contracting reading can justify smaller/safer CSP sizing even when the regime call above looks fine.
